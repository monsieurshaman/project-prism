--- ABOUT PRISM ---

Prism is a browser-based platform and Progressive Web App
(PWA) built around web technologies. It is designed to provide an
app-like experience through a compatible web browser, with
offline-capable functionality and installable PWA behavior where the
platform and browser support it.

Prism is not limited to one device manufacturer or operating system. A
device with a modern web browser can generally access the Prism website.
However, access does not mean every platform receives the same
capabilities.

DEVICE SUPPORT POLICY Prism distinguishes between website access and
full platform support.

1.  FULL SUPPORT — ANDROID: Android devices receive full support. Users
    can access Prism through a compatible modern browser. Supported
    Android browser and operating-system capabilities enable Prism’s
    intended PWA experience, including installation and standalone use
    where available. Offline behavior and other browser-dependent
    features require the relevant browser capabilities and successful
    caching.

2.  FULL SUPPORT — WINDOWS: Windows devices receive full support. Users
    can access Prism through a compatible modern desktop browser.
    Supported browsers can provide the intended web experience and PWA
    installation/standalone behavior where available. Offline behavior
    and other browser-dependent features require the relevant browser
    capabilities and successful caching.

3.  LIMITED EXPERIENCE — APPLE DEVICES: Apple devices, including
    supported iPhone, iPad, and Mac devices, can visit Prism through a
    web browser. They are not classified as receiving the full Prism
    experience. Some features, installation flows, standalone behavior,
    offline capabilities, or browser integrations may be limited or
    behave differently depending on the Apple device, operating-system
    version, and browser. Prism remains accessible on these devices, but
    users should expect a limited experience rather than feature parity
    with Android and Windows.

4.  OTHER DEVICES AND PLATFORMS: Other devices with a modern browser may
    be able to open and use the Prism website. Compatibility and
    available features depend on the browser, operating system, device
    capabilities, and support for modern web standards. These devices
    are not automatically considered fully supported.

[BROWSER AND INSTALLATION NOTES] - Prism is accessed through
https://proprism.pages.dev/. - A modern browser with support for the web
technologies used by Prism is recommended. - PWA installation
availability depends on the browser and operating system. An install
option may not appear on every device or browser. - Installing Prism is
not required to visit the website. - Prism’s offline-capable behavior
depends on service worker support, successful resource caching, and the
browser’s storage policies. - Browser updates, private-browsing modes,
storage restrictions, network conditions, and device settings may affect
functionality. - Feature availability can change as Prism and supported
browsers evolve.

[SUPPORT SUMMARY] Android: Full support. Windows: Full support. Apple
devices: Website access available; limited experience; not full support.
Other browser-capable devices: Website access may be available; feature
support varies.

[IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION] “Can visit Prism” does not mean “fully supported
by Prism.” Full support refers to the intended overall Prism experience,
not merely the ability to load the website. Apple device users are not
blocked from accessing Prism. Their experience is simply more limited
and may differ from Android and Windows. No specific browser version or
device model is guaranteed by this document.
