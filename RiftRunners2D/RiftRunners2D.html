The Six Phases
STABILIZE — Standard spawn rate, light walls.
COLLAPSE — Denser walls, tougher hostiles.
EXPANSION — Fast swarm, minimal cover.
ANOMALY — Boss phase. The Anomaly shifts between single / shotgun / bazooka / grenade fire and can mind-control you.
MOON — Take -40% damage, enemies slow down. Rare post-ANOMALY reprieve.
BLOODMOON — Take +50% damage, enemies speed up. Rare post-ANOMALY surge.
Weapons Arsenal
[1] Zip Zapper — 20 ammo, rapid single shots. Reliable baseline.
[2] Tactical Shotgun — 10 ammo, 5-pellet spread. Devastating up close.
[3] Bazooka — 5 ammo, rocket with wide AoE. Slow but heavy.
[4] Grenade — 4 ammo, fused AoE explosion. Perfect for clusters.
Combo Stacks
Chain kills with the same weapon to stack damage bonuses (max 5). Swapping weapons or 3s of idle time resets the combo.
Zapper +20% · Shotgun +10% · Bazooka +70% · Grenade +60% per stack.
Echo Protocol
Press E to drop an echo clone that replays your last 10 seconds, auto-firing at nearby hostiles. Each echo lasts 4s; charges recharge over 10s. Max charges scale with shop upgrades.
Shards & Shop
Earn shards from kills (score ÷ 100 on death), early phase clears (+100), and boss kills (+100 to +300).
Upgrades reset to zero on death — spend before you die.
Instant powerups are in-run only (max 4 per type per run).
Survival Tips
▸ Dash sideways to dodge boss lunges and AoE blasts — the i-frames are real.
▸ Weapon-carrying enemies telegraph aim tier. Bad = spray, Excellent = deadly accurate.
▸ Mind Controlled? Mash SPACE 5 times to break free — nothing else works.
▸ Frozen enemies deal no contact damage. Freeze the melee crowd, then focus-fire the shooters.
▸ BLOODMOON imminent? Consider retreating to cover before the surge hits.
