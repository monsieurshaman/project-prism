PROJECT PRISM — ABOUT
=====================

Project Prism is the name and branding for a small collaborative project group, as well as the Progressive Web App (PWA) that serves as the group's central hub.

Prism is the front door and framework for the projects collected under the brand. It brings together games, experiments, web apps, and practical tools in one place. The things inside Prism are sub-apps/projects; they do not all need to be flagship projects to belong there. Prism is not itself just one game or one standalone product.

The home page describes Prism as a central hub where the group's experiments, games, and handy tools live together. Its visible library is organized into:
- Games
- Apps & oddities
- Tools

FLAGSHIP PROJECTS
-----------------

The two main flagship projects are:

- SB3D — Sand Box 3D, an interactive 3D physics playground.
- RR2D — Rift Runners 2D, a top-down survival game. The current home-page listing labels it PC-only.

These are the main flagships, not the entire definition or limit of Project Prism. Other smaller sub-apps, experiments, and utilities can live inside the Prism hub without being flagships.

PROJECT STRUCTURE AND POSITIONING
---------------------------------

- "Project Prism" is the group's umbrella name/branding.
- "Prism" is the PWA and central hub.
- Sub-apps are the individual projects and experiences hosted or linked through that hub.
- Flagship status is a distinction, not a membership requirement: a sub-app can be part of Prism without being a flagship.
- SB3D and RR2D are the two main flagships.
- The hub currently groups its collection into games, apps & oddities, and tools.
